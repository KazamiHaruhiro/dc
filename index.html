Drive Collector

A private, single-operator tool for organising the owner’s own Google Drive storage.

Home Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

Drive Collector is used by its owner to organise, de-duplicate and audit files that the owner already stores in the owner’s own Google Drive accounts. It reads and manages only files that belong to the owner.

Who uses it

A single operator — the owner of the Google account(s) that authorise the application. There is no sign-up, no public access, and no third-party users.

Scope of access

The application requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope so it can list, verify, rename, de-duplicate and organise files in the authorising account. All processing happens on infrastructure controlled by the operator.
