Drive Collector

A private, single-operator tool for managing the owner's own Google Drive storage.

Home Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

Drive Collector is used by its owner to organise, de-duplicate and audit files that the owner already stores in the owner's own Google Drive accounts. It reads and manages only files that belong to the owner.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of the Google account(s) that authorise the application. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no third parties.
